## What Pro includes, and where it stops

Pro is the paid tier for the Claude chat product, and it covers a lot more than a bigger message allowance. You get Claude Code for terminal-based coding work, Claude Science, Projects, access to more models, Claude in Chrome for browser work, Claude for Microsoft 365, task handoff where Claude returns finished documents, slides, sheets and designs, early access to new features, and priority access when traffic is heavy. Claude.com also lists Fable in the Pro tier, paid with usage credits, and Claude Dashboards in beta.

The gap worth knowing before you pay: **Pro does not include API usage through the Claude Console.** If you want the chat product and programmatic access, those are two separate bills. You set up Console access and pay for API tokens on top of the subscription. People who subscribe expecting API calls to be covered are the ones who end up surprised at the end of the month.

## How the usage limits work, and what happens when you hit one

Pro runs two limits at once. One is a 5-hour session limit, the other is a weekly limit that applies across all models.

The weekly one behaves differently from what most people assume. It resets at a fixed day and time assigned to your account, and that slot stays the same whether you subscribed on a Tuesday or started a heavy session at midnight. You get the full weekly allowance each cycle, and the next reset time is visible in Settings > Usage. Changing when you work doesn't move the reset earlier.

Anthropic doesn't publish a message count for any tier. How fast you burn through an allowance depends on message length, the size of files you attach, how long the current conversation has run, and which model or feature you're using. Long attachments inside a long conversation with a heavier model drain a limit far faster than short prompts.

Two tools exist for the moment you hit a wall. Pro subscribers can enable usage credits to keep working past the included allowance, paying for the extra usage. Separately, if you have a limit reset available, you can use it to restore either the 5-hour or the weekly limit to full instead of waiting. Anthropic also reserves the right to apply additional caps on models or features at its discretion, and it doesn't guarantee uninterrupted service.

## Is the annual Pro plan worth $200?

Run the arithmetic on the two billing options. Monthly billing is $20 a month, so twelve months cost $240. Annual billing is a single $200 charge, which saves $40 over the year and works out to about $16.67 a month. The $17 figure you see quoted is that annual rate spread across twelve months, not a monthly charge. If you sign up annually, $200 leaves your account once.

That structure makes the decision simple. If you've been using Claude for two or three months and expect to keep going, the annual plan saves you money with no loss of features. If you're testing whether the paid tier fits your workflow, or you might switch to Max partway through the year, pay monthly and keep the flexibility.

One detail for anyone planning to scale up later: if you move from an annual Pro plan to Max, the unused balance on your Pro plan is credited to your account and applied to future subscription costs.

## Max or Ultra: what the extra $100 buys

Max starts at $100 a month and gives you 5x Pro usage in both the 5-hour window and the weekly window, plus early access to new models and features and priority access during peak traffic. The entry tier also includes $100 a month in API credits.

Ultra is $200 a month and adds three things: no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, 10x Pro usage every week, and $200 a month in API credits instead of $100.

The API credits are the part that changes the math. If you already spend $150 a month on API usage, Ultra's $200 credit covers that spend and the extra usage capacity comes along with it. If you never touch the API, you're paying for a credit you won't draw down, and Max covers most heavy chat workloads.

Those credits have rules attached. The monthly API credits bundled with Max, Ultra and Team plans aren't purchased, so they can't be refunded. They expire at the end of each billing cycle, which means unused credits don't roll over. Cancelling or refunding your plan stops new credits from being issued, though credits you already hold stay usable until they expire.

## Discount codes: what exists and what doesn't

Anthropic states plainly that it doesn't offer standard discounted pricing for individual plans, meaning Pro, Max and Ultra. Its support team cannot issue one-off discounts or coupons, so any page promising a working Claude promo code for a personal plan is either out of date, pointing at a limited promotion that has ended, or inventing the code.

What does exist: occasional limited-time promotions announced through Anthropic's official channels, and discounted pricing on Team and Enterprise plans for eligible nonprofit organizations. If a coupon site asks you to complete a survey or hand over payment details to reveal a code, there is nothing behind it.

## Cancelling and managing the subscription

Every plan is listed as no commitment, cancel anytime. You can cancel from the web or desktop app by clicking your name in the lower-left corner, opening Settings, going to Billing, and clicking Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access to the paid plan until then.

To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before your next billing date. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, the subscription is managed by that store rather than through Claude's billing settings, and cancellation goes through the store's subscription page.

## Which Claude plan to pick

Three rules cover almost every case:

**You want to test the paid experience with real work.** Take Pro monthly at $20. You get Claude Code, Projects, the higher allowance and the integrations, and you can stop after a month.

**You already use Claude daily and hit the 5-hour cap regularly.** Move to Max. The 5x allowance in both windows is the difference between finishing a task and waiting for a reset.

**You run long sessions all day, or you spend real money on the API.** Ultra. The missing 5-hour limit and the $200 monthly API credit are what you're paying for, and either one alone can justify the price.

Before you pay, confirm two things: that you're in a supported location, since Claude's paid plans aren't available everywhere, and whether tax is included in the price you're shown or added at checkout. Both change the amount that actually leaves your account.
